# Repository maintenance

Before editing a workflow, read [docs/DEBUGGING.md](docs/DEBUGGING.md) and the relevant entry in [docs/BUG_LEDGER.md](docs/BUG_LEDGER.md). Preserve unrelated work and existing identity, expiry, patient-grant and unknown-nutrient safeguards. For a reproduced bug, add a focused failing regression, record verification limits and update the ledger. Do not store credentials or real clinical records in debugging evidence.
